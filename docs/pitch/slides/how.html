HOW IT WORKS

From raw data to measured improvement.

1 · DATA IN

API or manual entry

Tools push KPIs through a secure API with retries and backdating; teams can also enter values by hand.

2 · SCORE

Targets and confidence

Each value is judged against its target, stale inputs are left out, scores show their confidence.

3 · ACT

Alerts and actions

Off-target KPIs raise alerts by bell, e-mail or Slack; leads turn them into owned actions.

4 · PROVE

Measured outcomes

Each action’s metric is re-checked at completion, 14 and 30 days: improved, unchanged or worse.

The loop is the point: not just a dashboard, but a way to show that the quality work actually moved a number.
